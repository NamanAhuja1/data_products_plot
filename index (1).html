Horsepower and fuel economy

27 October 2026

The question

Do cars with more horsepower use more fuel?

I used the 32 cars in R’s built-in mtcars dataset to compare engine horsepower with miles per gallon (mpg). A higher mpg means better fuel economy.

The chart

Each dot is a car. Hover to see its name and values. You can also zoom in or hide a cylinder group in the legend.

What I noticed

Cars with higher horsepower generally have lower mpg in this group. The four-cylinder cars mostly sit toward the upper left of the chart, while the eight-cylinder cars cluster farther right and lower down.

This is a pattern in these 32 cars, not proof that horsepower alone causes the difference. Weight and other design choices matter too.

Data: mtcars, included with R. The data come from the 1974 Motor Trend US magazine road tests.
